How it works

  1. 1

    Run the command

    The script picks the binary for your CPU, checks its checksum and opens a menu in your terminal.

  2. 2

    Pick a suite

    Benchpress fetches the pinned compiler and sources into its own cache, then times clean and incremental builds several times each.

  3. 3

    See where you stand

    Compare your times with machines like yours. Publish to get a page you can share, or keep the result local.
