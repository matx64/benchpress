Compile-time benchmarks

How fast does your machine build code?

Benchpress builds real open source projects with pinned compilers, times every build, and shows where your machine ranks against others. You need one command and no compiler, and results stay on your machine unless you publish them.

{{ install_box("install-cmd") }}

Linux (x86_64, arm64) and Apple Silicon Macs. No sudo, nothing left behind. What the script does

1

Run the command

The script checks your CPU, downloads the matching binary, verifies its checksum, and opens a menu in your terminal.

2

Pick a suite

Benchpress downloads the exact compiler and sources the suite pins into its own cache, then times clean and incremental builds several times each. Compilers you already have are never used or changed.

3

See where you stand

Compare your times with machines like yours. Publish the result to get a shareable page, or keep it local.
